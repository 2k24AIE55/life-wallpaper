# The Life Wallpaper

Your life in weeks — one dot per week — drawn quietly on your Android home screen.

**The Life Wallpaper** is a free Android live wallpaper built around the 2,000-year-old idea of *memento mori*: remember that your time is finite. Enter your date of birth and the app renders your life as a minimalist grid of dots. Filled dots are the weeks you've lived; empty dots are the ones ahead. It updates on its own as weeks pass.

- Custom colors for lived weeks, remaining weeks, and background
- Live preview before applying
- Automatic weekly updates — zero maintenance
- No account, no ads, no notifications
- Battery-friendly: static grid, redrawn only when the week changes
