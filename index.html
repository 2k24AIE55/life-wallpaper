What is it?

The Life Wallpaper is a free Android live wallpaper built around a 2,000-year-old idea: memento mori — remember that your time is finite. Enter your date of birth and the app renders your life as a minimalist grid of dots. Filled dots are the weeks you've already lived; empty dots are the ones ahead. The wallpaper updates on its own as weeks pass, so the picture stays honest without you doing anything.

Why a wallpaper?

Ambient, not another app to open

Every productivity app lives behind an icon you have to remember to tap. Your wallpaper meets you in the half-second before you open social media on autopilot — exactly when a quiet nudge matters most. Zero willpower required.

A number is abstract. A grid is visceral.

"2,340 weeks remaining" is easy to ignore. A grid of thousands of dots — a small filled corner and a vast empty field — you feel before you think about. That's the whole trick.

Lightweight by design

A live wallpaper has one unforgivable sin: draining your battery. The grid is static, redrawn only when something actually changes, like a new week ticking over.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed.

Does it drain battery?

No. The wallpaper is a static grid that only redraws when the week changes.

Where does my birthdate go?

It stays on your device — it's only used to compute the grid.
